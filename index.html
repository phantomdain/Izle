From Me, To You

As I step closer to 18, I find myself caught between two skies — one reflecting cherished memories, while the other holds dreams yet to unfold. This milestone marks more than the turning of a chapter; it promises a horizon ahead, where the lessons of my yesterdays meet the wonders of my tomorrows.
