Dress Code

Attire Guide


Ladies

A breezy dress or skirt ensemble in light yellow, champagne, sage green, and dusty pink. Sandals or espadrilles, with light, playful accessories.


Gents

Polo and light trousers, or a summer suit in champagne, sage green, midnight, and navy blue. Loafers or boat shoes, with simple, elegant accessories.

Please Note:

Jeans are strictly not allowed.
We kindly ask everyone to follow the suggested attire to help maintain the elegance and theme of the celebration.


A Note on Gifts

In lieu of traditional gifts, a contribution to my educational fund would be greatly appreciated.

Your presence at my debut is the greatest gift I could ask for. While I genuinely appreciate any thoughtful material gifts, I would be especially grateful for a monetary contribution toward my college education.
